# topo v0.4.0

Motion in the desktop app is rebuilt on springs: everything that moves can be interrupted and reversed mid-flight, surfaces leave the way they came, and the canvas answers gestures with momentum. The GUI also draws large graphs faster.

## Motion

- Menus, dropdowns, dialogs, toasts and the save status now scale and fade in and out. A surface that is leaving no longer takes clicks.
- Buttons shrink slightly while pressed.
- The theme menu's System / Light / Dark thumb and the Monotone switch slide.
- With "Reduce motion" on (macOS setting, or `TOPO_REDUCE_MOTION=1`), movement and scaling are skipped and only short fades remain.

## Canvas

- Camera moves (fit, reveal, frame selection) keep their velocity when a new move starts before the last one ends.
- Mouse-wheel zoom steps accumulate smoothly and stay anchored at the pointer.
- Pinching past the zoom limits is resisted and springs back on release. Pinching during a camera move no longer jumps.
- Releasing a drag-pan with speed lets the canvas glide to a stop; any press stops it.
- When the layout changes (adding, deleting, undo, hiding completed work, grouping by tag, folding a group), cards move to their new places and edges follow. New cards grow in; deleted cards fade out.
- Selection, dimming and completing a task crossfade, and the check mark pops.
- Large graphs render faster: rows outside the viewport are skipped and dashed edges are clipped to the view.

## Editing

- Choice lists scroll through every entry with the wheel and show a scroll bar.
- The caret stays visible while composing text with an input method, and long titles no longer shift while being edited.
- The save status floats over the canvas instead of pushing it.
- The Jev organize controls are shown only when available, and long errors wrap.
- The toolbar's Monotone option is a switch, and the brand mark is gone from the toolbar.

## Known limits

- On a layout change in a very large graph (thousands of cards), the single frame that recomputes the layout can take longer than one display frame. The animation after it is smooth.
- Text is slightly soft while a surface is scaling.

## Notes for upgrading / アップグレード時の注意

Existing workspaces and settings are kept.

既存のワークスペースと設定はそのまま引き継がれます。アニメーションを減らしたい場合は、macOS の「視差効果を減らす」を有効にするか、環境変数 `TOPO_REDUCE_MOTION=1` を設定してください。

## Installation / インストール

Download the installer for your platform from the assets below and verify it against `SHA256SUMS`. The macOS `.pkg` and the Windows setup executable are not signed by Apple or Microsoft; see [macOS installation](https://github.com/r4ai/topo/blob/main/docs/macos-install.md) if the installer is blocked.

お使いの環境に合うインストーラーを下のアセットからダウンロードしてください。macOS の `.pkg` と Windows のセットアップは署名されていないため、ブロックされた場合は上記リンクの手順で許可してください。
